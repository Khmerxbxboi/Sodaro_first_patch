    UX & UI case study

    Designed around real Oakland decisions.

    Sodaro turns neighborhood conditions into a clear path from awareness to action. The experience is built for busy owners, community members, immigrant families, elderly residents, and people who are not technical.

    01

    Research

    Field observation, local business pain points, neighborhood change, and civic-information gaps.

    02

    Structure

    Dashboard hierarchy, clear navigation, plain-language alerts, and reusable information cards.

    03

    Interaction

    See the condition, understand the cause, receive a suggestion, and take the next step.

    04

    Validation

    Usability testing, cognitive walkthroughs, observation, and iterative Figma revisions.

    Interaction design thesis

    From confusion to action.

    The core journey is simple: see the current status, understand the cause, receive a clear suggestion, and take action. Sodaro is designed for owners who do not have time to study complicated dashboards.

    1. 1See Slow, Normal, or Busy
    2. 2Understand the real-world cause
    3. 3Receive a practical recommendation
    4. 4Take action with confidence
