Solo graduate thesis

Interaction design built around clarity.

Sodaro explores how UX, UI, AI, and local context can help non-technical business owners make better real-world decisions.

The experience is intentionally simple: open the app, see the current state, understand the cause, receive a recommendation, and take action.
