Project overview

Sodaro connects business needs with city information.

My thesis asks a simple question: how can interaction design make local government information more useful to the people who need it?

ProblemRules and resources are spread across many sites and written in difficult language.
SolutionA clear AI-assisted experience organized around business tasks, location, and current city codes.
New featureUsers can photograph a storefront or workspace to identify possible inspection concerns and review relevant code guidance.
Next stepContinue testing with business owners and develop verified city-data partnerships.
